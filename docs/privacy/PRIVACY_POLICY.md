# PurgeQuest Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026
**Developer:** Joseph Dabrowski
**Contact:** PurgeQuestPhotoClean@outlook.com

PurgeQuest is a game that helps you clean up your photo library. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: **PurgeQuest does not collect, transmit or share any personal
data.**

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It does not send your photos, videos or their details (dates, locations, file names) anywhere. The app
  contains no networking code.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party software.
- It does not track you, and it does not share data with data brokers or other companies.

## Your photos and videos

PurgeQuest asks for access to your Photo Library so you can battle photo and video "monsters" and reclaim
storage. Photos and videos are analysed **on your device only**. When you confirm a deletion, the items are
moved to the Photos app's **Recently Deleted** album, from which you can restore them for 30 days. The app
never adds anything to your library.

## What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app (Settings also has a full
reset):

- game progress: level, gems, streak, cosmetics you own and quests;
- a short history of deletions (an identifier, size and date for each item);
- which items you chose to spare;
- cached fingerprints used to find duplicates (a small hash per item, not the image itself).

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The developer does not see your payment details. Apple may give
the developer standard sales reports that do not identify you personally. The app stores which items you
own on your device only.

## On-device text

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, short lines of flavour text may be written by Apple's
on-device model. This happens on your device and nothing is sent to the developer or any server.

## Children

PurgeQuest does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before that
version is released, and the effective date above will change.
