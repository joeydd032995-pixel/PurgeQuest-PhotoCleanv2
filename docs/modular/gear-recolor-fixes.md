# Gear recolor fixes

All **99 held/store gear assets** have regenerated material masks and source tones. The correction covers the reported Verdant Coil and Tidecaller staves, the 40 shield variants, and the same defects in other staves, signature weapons, and store items.

## Problem and resulting behavior

The previous generator clustered source colors. A light reflection could become a separate material from the surface beneath it; dark wood could be mistaken for outline; a thick outline or opaque drop shadow could become paint. Examples were partially brown Templar Aegis planks, the blue/red caps above shield rims, split-color staff shafts and crystal reflections, and arbitrary colors on different sides of one shield frame.

`gear_materials.py` instead declares the physical surfaces: continuous shafts/crooks, orb/crystal heads, blade faces, shield panels, rims, crosses, bosses, handles, and actual luminous inlays. Outline-bounded cells align those surfaces with the art. Unoutlined store gear uses the exact contours from `store_gear.py`. Reflected light, wood grain, engraved lines and paint shading remain within the same material. Genuine transparent tip emission remains independently colorable.

Ink, the opaque exterior shield shadow and disconnected antialias islands receive zero mask weight. This removes bright protruding caps without modifying the source silhouettes or alpha. The same arbitrary user color picker and shading-preserving recolor math apply; blue/red/gold are review preferences only.

## Material controls

- Orb/crystal-headed Verdant Coil, Tidecaller, Bone Reliquary and Voidspike: Primary head; Secondary continuous shaft. Carved patterns on the shaft do not create random color regions.
- Crook/fork staves: Primary continuous body; Secondary distinct gem where present. Root ornaments can expose Trim.
- Shields: Primary face/planks; Secondary separate frame/crossbar; Trim independent boss/ornament. A single embossed plate remains one material across its shine bands.
- Weapons/store gear: blade/head, hilt, grip, decorations and actual Glow regions follow their source surfaces. Available regions vary with the asset.

## Reproduce and validate

```sh
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/held_gear.py --masks-only --no-sheets
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/approvals.py apply
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/checklist.py
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/validate.py
python3 -W ignore::ResourceWarning -I tools/modular_assets/tests/test_modular_assets.py
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/gear_recolor_review.py
python3 -I tools/modular_assets/gear_fix_review.py
```

The comparison command needs the baseline commit in local Git history. The standalone original/recolored gallery does not.

The validator checks emitted mask coverage, protected ink/shadow, original alpha, partition overlap, region tone records, resources and fitting/catalog constraints. Source-art regression tests cover wood light/dark pigments, Verdant shaft shading, Tidecaller crystal reflections, shield-rim light bands, dark signature blades, bosses, and source ink/alpha under strongly contrasting preferences. Original sprites, source dimensions, grip/pivot/fit/animation data, catalog IDs, ownership, prices and save schema are retained.

These are texture/material reference renders. Existing per-asset human approval and native/device visual gates remain in place; no approval records are generated by the fix.
